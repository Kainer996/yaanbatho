Cybercity

A dense Three.js neon city: orbit, pan, zoom, dive between towers, and follow the airborne traffic.

LIVE SCENE
Procedural towers, window grids, sky lanes, rain volume, animated billboards, drones, rail loops, fog bloom, and moving camera bookmarks.

Navigation

  • Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll/pinch to zoom.
  • WASD glides the camera target through streets.
  • Q/E lower/raise the view. R resets to skyline.
  • Buttons on the right jump between hand-picked city viewpoints.
