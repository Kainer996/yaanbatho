What this is

Nomad is a tiny family of tools that respect driving cognition. Short. Voiced where it can be. One tap where it can't. The point isn't to replace Google Maps — it's to give you the answer that makes opening Maps a one-second job instead of a ten-minute spiral.

Everything works in a normal browser, no app install. Open it on your phone, save it to your home screen, you're done. The Trip Planner has an MCP server so any agent (Claude Desktop, OpenClaw, Claude Code) can drive it for you.
