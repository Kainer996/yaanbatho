## Overview

Nomad Travel Toolkit is a small ecosystem of parking-aware travel tools. It respects driving cognition — short, voiced, single-tap. The point isn't to replace Google Maps, it's to make opening Maps a one-second job instead of a ten-minute spiral.

## What's in it

### Trip Planner
Set your anchor (postcode, place, or GPS), drive-time budget, and vibe. Get three parking-aware destinations with weather (live Open-Meteo), daylight (NOAA-approximation, no API), parkup notes, and a Google Maps deeplink. Reads the brief aloud via browser TTS so you can hear the plan without looking at the screen. Available as an MCP server for Claude Desktop / OpenClaw / Claude Code.

### Pub Guide
Curated UK pubs with useful parking filtered by what matters tonight — parking, food, real ale, dog-friendly, garden, log fire, family room. One tap opens directions.

### Roll Call
Drop a pin where you parked up. Notes, vibe, weather, GPS reverse-geocoded place name. Local-only log book today; multi-user nomad feed coming with Pro.

## Why

The idea came from a simple travel problem: "where should I go for an hour?" Nomad collapses that to one paragraph and one tap.
