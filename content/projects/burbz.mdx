## Overview

Burbz is a web-based game where players identify real birds in the wild using sound recording or photo capture, then collect them as stylized manga fighter characters to battle against opponents.

## How It Works

### Identify
Record birdsong with your microphone or snap a photo. The app identifies the species using AI-powered recognition (BirdNET integration planned for sound, image classifiers for photos).

### Collect
Each identified bird becomes a unique fighter character in your Flock. Birds have stats (HP, ATK, DEF, SPD, SPL), rarity tiers (Common to Legendary), and four combat moves based on their real-world behaviour.

### Battle
Choose a bird from your Flock and enter the arena. Turn-based combat with VS intro screens, hit effects, damage numbers, KO animations, and XP/leveling progression.

## Visual Style

Manga/Street Fighter-inspired dark UI with:
- Hand-crafted SVG fighter portraits for each species
- Speed lines, glow effects, and impact animations
- Rarity-coded card borders and color schemes

## Current Roster

16 species including European Robin, Peregrine Falcon, Golden Eagle, Barn Owl, Common Raven, Kingfisher, Puffin, and more. Each with unique archetype (Speedster, Bruiser, Assassin, Mage, Trickster, Tank).
