Night Run 0: Launchpad

This first page is the scaffold for the overnight build sprint. The goal is to turn each hour into a concrete product artefact: a researched opportunity, a simple build, a live page, and an Obsidian note.

Selection principles:

  • Can plausibly earn, attract leads, or create leverage for Yaan.
  • Small enough to prototype overnight.
  • Connected to Yaan's actual strengths: AI, music, animals, van life, plant/quarry knowledge, creative tools, and direct human usefulness.
  • No doom, no war/politics/death content. We build life-forward things.
