Why this matters

Dog owners spend heavily on care, travel and convenience, but on real days — bad weather, van moves, campsite mornings, work calls — they need quick calm activities more than theory. This is useful, kind and search-friendly.

Monetisation

The free planner can lead to printable enrichment cards, a 30-day calm dog calendar, a van-dog packing checklist, and affiliate links for snuffle mats, lick mats, cooling mats, collapsible bowls, travel crates and GPS tags.
