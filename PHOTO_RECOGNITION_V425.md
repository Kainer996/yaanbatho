# Photo recognition v425

Ordinary phone photos were rejected by a prompt that treated blur as failure, an absolute `clear` quality gate, and a second crop whose coordinates could be applied to the wrong image. The current Gemini 2.5 Flash baseline also confidently called the Carrion Crow fixture American Crow.

The replacement uses Gemini 3.8 Flash and a field-identification prompt. Quality and identification confidence are separate: two agreeing views with visible diagnostic features, original-pixel subject checks, confidence ≥0.80 and a ≥0.20 gap between different taxa are required for a confirmed discovery. A critical second pass sees both original and crop; the original subject box remains authoritative for pixel checks. Scientific names distinguish taxa while common-name synonyms no longer count as competing species. A policy-specific photo digest prevents stale prior-model receipts being replayed as new results.

An uncertain result can show up to three clearly labelled possible species. It never reaches discovery/reward handling. Service failure cannot supply suggestions, and every result still needs the current model/policy and a valid worker receipt. Error wording no longer falsely promises permanent photo storage: the direct camera/library flow retains a retry image only in memory.

Seventeen distinct photos were evaluated: seven correct confirmed identifications, five unconfirmed bird photos, and five rejected negative images. No wrong species was confirmed by the final algorithm. The original wagtail now confirms Grey Wagtail; the original woodpecker yields a correct Great Spotted Woodpecker suggestion but remains unconfirmed. Gull discrimination is incomplete: the California gull remains unconfirmed and its tentative European Herring Gull suggestion is wrong. This is a finite regression sample, not an accuracy guarantee. An earlier candidate with a wrong confirmed gull was rejected and never published.

Validation: 80 Python checks,17 client/crop/result checks,10 real browser/IndexedDB queue scenarios,10 native photo-interface groups, and an actual v424→v425 service-worker update followed by offline restart retaining the existing save and new contract. Five optional live-route snapshot tests were skipped locally; the guarded installer separately proves live upload cleanup, exact loaded hashes and unchanged sound before promoting the shell. Sanitized results: `reports/BURBZ_V425_RECOGNITION_VERIFICATION.json`. Public verification remains pending.

The original persistent £5 monthly ledger remains unchanged. Prices conservatively use the published full Gemini3.8 Flash standard rates ($1.50 input/$7.50 output per million), ignoring the temporary discount, plus the existing GBP conversion/tax allowance. Reservations cover two calls including thoughts; paid retries are never automatic. Pricing expires under the existing review date. Model behavior and thinking/output limits were checked against [Google image documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-understanding) and [pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing).

No private diagnostic routes, original private photos, raw response traces, unapproved inventory panels or replacement pasture artwork are included.
