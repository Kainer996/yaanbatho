POCKET PRODUCER

Type a vibe. Pick a style. Get a track. A free in-browser music maker — runs on Meta's open-source MusicGen, shaped by the signature sound of producers Kainer and Il Diablo.

↓ Download MP3

▸How it works

1
DESCRIBE

Type the vibe in plain English. The more specific, the better — instruments, mood, tempo, era.

2
PICK A STYLE

Three presets shape the prompt: Kainer (melodic techno), Il Diablo (tech house), or just-wing-it for no preset.

3
GENERATE

Meta's open-source MusicGen model renders the audio in 20-30 seconds. Play it, download it, share it.

▸Trained on real catalogues

The style presets are not generic. They are built from two working UK producers' own output — the prompt prefix injects their signature palette, BPM range, and key choices so the generator leans toward sounds that actually exist on their records.
