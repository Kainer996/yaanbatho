## Attribution decision

Official OpenFreeMap guidance requires attribution. OpenMapTiles’ [licence](https://github.com/openmaptiles/openmaptiles/blob/master/LICENSE.md) asks for visible OpenMapTiles credit and corner attribution on browsable maps; no exception was requested or assumed. [OSMF guidance](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines) permits collapsible interactive-map credit after initial display and separate credits for games. We therefore retain discoverable compact map credit rather than silently removing the providers. [MapLibre’s native AttributionControl](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/API/classes/AttributionControl/) is used on browsable maps. Full notices are reachable from Settings and each control. Existing terrain/source notices and external catalogue links are preserved; direct catalogue fetch returned HTTP 403 in this environment.
