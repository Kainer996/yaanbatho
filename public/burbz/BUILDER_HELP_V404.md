# Help the builder v404

At an active village or town-ward scaffold, walk within three metres of its actual footprint and tap **Help the builder** three times (or use F). This saves one reduction equal to 25% of the project's original duration. Walking away, flying, entering a room or opening a modal cancels partial help. Known ground and a clear approach are required. The one-use receipt remains in the canonical village project across reload and scene rebuild; coins and inventory are not spent. A failed durable save restores the same state objects and allows a retry.

The action uses the existing walking frame at four updates per second, caches actual scaffold bounds, rejects distant sites before terrain queries, and removes its listeners/DOM on teardown. It also reads streamed destination buildings through the existing continuity interface. It adds no terrain, render loop, rewards or combat authority. Touch controls remain reachable at 320×568, 390×844, 667×375 and 1280×800. Near a scaffold F helps; elsewhere its existing flight behavior remains.
