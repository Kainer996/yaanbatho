# Discovery rewards and card Personality

Pending integration by the sole release owner. Base: `dd3a0626d685b9bba22784c2f14341a8d6f93445`; discovery commit: `e9cb680`. No release/build/cache pins or runtime module URLs changed. The release must append its own build/cache stamp so existing installs receive the inline game changes.

Accepted recognitions now use the persisted Birdex identity to decide first discovery. That first discovery retains the normal 8 player XP, rarity coins, announcement and single Birdex species. Repeated detections, including recruited birds, add no player or companion XP and no discovery toast/encounter banner. The accepted-detection funnel covers continuous sound, one-shot sound and photo routes. Canonical aliases, legacy IDs/scientific names, and companions without a discovery row count as known. The record is claimed synchronously before reward callbacks can re-enter. Existing alias indices remain part of the save format; they are not additional Birdex species.

Re-sightings retain sighting counts, best confidence, last-seen time, recruitment discounts and existing photo metadata. The sound shelf remains one tile per species with a brief 420ms icon fade; active fades are canceled rather than queued, and reduced motion suppresses the fade. Explicit recruitment and quest actions retain their own rewards. No discoveries or earned XP are erased.

Personality is the existing `cha`/Charm mechanic, not a second stat. Full equipment cards, companion/Birdex reverse cards, field guide and sound discovery backs expose it by name. Goldcrest's species Charm default rises from 4/10 to 9/10 (90 at level 1), an explicit game-design choice. Local and cloud flock hydration raise old Goldcrests to the corresponding level/training-aware minimum, preserve any higher trained CHA, fill missing CHA, and leave other stats, XP, health, identity, equipment and bond unchanged. Existing civic roles consume that same CHA; their formula is unchanged.
