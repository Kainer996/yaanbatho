# Magpie Market Buy and Sell — v389

The counter has separate keyboard-accessible Buy and Sell tabs. Buy lists all crafting materials and Kitchen larder ingredients, priced through the existing rarity markup and Market Trader discount. Sell lists positive owned stacks from the actual Stores bags: every collected Forge recipe (35 recipes across weapons, armour, trinkets, spells and potions), materials, food, scrolls and curios. Equipped pieces remain on their birds until explicitly unequipped. In-progress commissions become sellable when collected through the existing Forge flow.

Buy 1/5 and Sell 1/All update the actual inventory bags and purse. The full requested purchase must be affordable; a shortage offers the existing coin-earning dialog without a partial purchase. Sale quantities clamp to current stock, emptied stacks are removed, and stale or invalid actions pay nothing. Catalog identity, item category, integer quantities, the Market building gate and finite purse totals are rechecked at execution.

The shared Stores sale and counter purchase use the existing snapshot/restore and durable-save machinery. Inventory, coins and counter quest/trade progress commit together. On storage failure the same live state tree is restored, with no success effects; a retry can succeed once. Quest notices are deferred until after commit. Rendering replaces only the counter panel, preserves the active tab and screen scroll, and restores keyboard focus to the action or current tab when a sold row disappears. No new save keys or migrations are needed for tab state.

Only the HTML shell and service-worker epoch advance to `market-tabs-v389-20260910`. Existing module pins, including both landscape modules, remain unchanged. No new artwork or Merlin changes are included.
