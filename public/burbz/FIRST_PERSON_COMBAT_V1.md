# First-person wilderness combat and controls

The user's decision is resolved: zombies are encountered while walking **only in wilderness between villages and towns**. Homes, settlements, interiors and unknown map coverage remain nonhostile. Existing turn-based battles remain intact. This implementation belongs to dedicated task `01a08d0f-de5d-7883-9cbb-838a5468371b`; no replacement UI task is needed. Merlin 3D is paused and unrelated.

## Gameplay

- The Satchel equips actual owned/crafted copies from the same five-slot ledger as birds. All 35 Forge definitions remain equipable and protected from Market sales while worn. Atomic swaps, potion consumption/refills and failed-save rollback preserve item counts and object identity. Keeper equipment never completes a bird-equipment quest.
- All ten weapons attack: talons use the existing physical PECK rules within an aimed 2.4m reach; wands launch the existing magic SPARK projectile. The equipped weapon's real stats, crit and Forge tempering apply. Weapon and spell selection uses the two labelled buttons or keys 1/2.
- All five scrolls work: Ember Wisp fireball (35% splash), Tempest (55% splash), Frost (real speed debuff), Mending Light (real magic-scaled healing), Phoenix Chorus (healing and cleanse). Nearby living hostile targets within 3m take splash only with unobstructed line of sight. There are no allied companion fighters in walking combat; team-wide healing affects the lone player, not remote birds.
- `battle_core.resolveExplorationSkill` calls the same seeded damage, defence/resistance, critical, barrier, faint, rider and heal helpers as battles. It never calls a turn-based battle or grants battle/quest rewards. Armour protects against actual zombie attacks; enhancements affect readiness, damage and crit. Satchel carry bonuses retain their existing expedition meaning; no new walking capacity or reward system is invented.
- The player uses `buildFighter`'s existing default base stats, with real keeper name/level and equipment bonuses. HP is absolute across equipment swaps, so changing armour cannot heal. Confirmed settlement/home refuge restores HP and clears conditions. At zero HP the player can move back to safety; attacks stop. Unknown/loading terrain, flying and pausing cannot grant refuge healing. No currency death penalty or teleport is introduced.
- Real-time pacing adapts battle readiness: effective SPD fills 100 CR, at one battle tick per active second (default SPD 40 means 2.5 seconds). Release spends readiness even on a miss, and an equipped scroll starts its existing CD count in full readiness beats. Costs commit before launch; reloading cannot refund a shot. No mana/ammunition system exists in the definitions, so none is charged. Potions use the existing effects once per readiness beat, consuming/refilling the actual equipped copy atomically. Temporary effects and cooldown progress persist.
- The second attack stick sits above the ordinary right look stick. Hold/drag aims, release attacks. Separate pointer ownership supports simultaneous movement, look and aim. Q/arrow keys and right-mouse hold/drag/release are equivalents; Space/Enter on the focused attack button are accessible equivalents. Escape, pointer/touch cancel, capture loss, blur, hidden page, resize, menu, navigation and teardown cancel without firing.

## Host contract

`BurbzWildernessCombat.attach(s)` borrows `s.root`/`s.host`, `s.source.scene`/`s.scene`, `s.player`/`s.p`, dynamic `s.world`, `s.options.character().combat` and `s.abort.signal`. Call `update(dt)` once before camera/render; call `reset()` with existing input reset; `dispose()` before scene teardown (also abort-bound and idempotent). No new renderer, GL context, terrain or animation loop is created.

Owner-provided world methods take precedence: `safeAt(x,z)` returns explicit false only in confirmed wilderness; `refugeAt(x,z)` identifies actual healing refuges; `combatKey(x,z)` supplies a stable cell identity. Existing `height`, `allowed`, optional `combatAllowed3`/`allowed3` and `clear(a,b)` provide collision/LOS. Missing height and unknown boundaries fail safe.

For the release owner's current v391 retained renderer, the runtime directly supports `options.continuousWorld.record/anchor`, `scene.userData.walkSurface.radius`, dynamic `world.height`, and `character().combat.settlementsInBounds` (canonical `geographicSettlementRecords`). A bounded cached lookup protects all nearby villages/towns/cities, including unloaded settlement models, plus the complete authored starting settlement and home. Queries refresh after 80m, fail safe outside known coverage or on errors, and stop on disposal. This requires four lifecycle hooks and the Space/Enter button guard in `village_walk.js`; it does not replace the streaming terrain. The latest v391 flight collision requires full camera coverage, so its small `combatAllowed3` hook reuses identical tree/building collision while requiring only swept-point height coverage. The exact two-line patch is in the handoff directory. The older geographic route also has explicit safety/lifecycle hooks and actual encounters.

## Budgets and persistence

Four active zombies, eight swept projectiles, sixteen impact effects; projectiles expire at 3 active seconds, impacts at 0.45 seconds. Geometry/materials are shared, pools are reclaimed, and all owned resources dispose. AI uses bounded local steering and existing collision; projectiles use the loaded-point combat collision hook rather than the flight camera-coverage gate. Combat CPU samples retain at most 180 entries. `wildernessCombat` stores HP, shields, buffs, readiness/cooldowns, seeded RNG and at most 64 wounded/defeated cell receipts. Very old encounters can regenerate after those receipts rotate out; no rewards can be farmed because none are granted. Paused or closed scenes do not simulate offline attacks.
