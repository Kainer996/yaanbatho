# Area Birds v377

The geographic quest map now has one Area Birds button and no expanding species sidebar. It opens an independent, scrollable 2D field guide with all eligible supported local species, illustrated cards, scientific names, search, most-likely/rarest/alphabetical ordering and a Back to map button. Preview, Escape and Back preserve the page and return focus. Existing quest routes, camera, discoveries, rewards and saved progression stay authoritative.

## Evidence and limits

The list uses existing UK, Australia and mainland Spain/Balearic catalogue, range and season rules, plus recognised checklist species with actual local records. It is not capped at the old nine random spawns. Historic-only entries are excluded. Accepted scientific identities deduplicate aliases before combining range/season metadata. Unknown locations and unsupported regions have explicit empty states and make no record request.

Two public, credential-free GBIF occurrence queries cover a roughly 20 km polygon around a centre rounded to 0.1 degrees: one for the current calendar month, one for all months, across the previous five years plus the current year. Present human/machine observations require coordinates and no flagged geospatial issue. Exact device GPS is not sent. Scientific-name facets aggregate author/subspecies suffixes and reviewed synonyms; records are observations, not unique individuals or measured encounter probabilities.

Most likely first orders current-calendar-month historical record counts descending, using nearby mapped habitat to break ties. Without records it uses range, season and habitat, then alphabetical ties. Rarest first orders positive all-month record counts ascending; missing records come last because absence of reporting cannot establish rarity. Frequency labels explicitly describe record totals, not conservation status. Observer effort, access and reporting bias are explained in the page's methodology. No invented probability percentages or game rarity scores enter this order.

`area_birds_core.js` owns pure query/parser/ranking logic. `area_birds.js` cancels stale and hidden requests, has a 15-second timeout and explicit refresh/retry, and retains up to six coarse-area record caches for seven days. Fresh caches avoid requests for 24 hours. Corrupt/future/expired entries are rejected; failed refresh keeps usable previous evidence or clearly labels habitat/season estimates. Records are separate from game saves and never grant sightings, discoveries or rewards.

`area_birds_taxonomy.js` is generated by `node scripts/build_area_birds_taxonomy.cjs`. Inputs are committed catalogue/education/national profiles and the accepted BirdLife AU fixture for three base profiles without scientific fields. Public GBIF species matches are cached in `TAXONOMY_CACHE` (default `/root/burbz-area-birds-v377-evidence/taxonomy-cache`); `--offline` rebuilds from that cache. 1,594 common-name entries represent 1,542 source scientific names; 1,529 match exact species/subspecies identities or six explicitly reviewed spelling variants. Thirteen newer/split identities retain their original names and visibly say record matching is unverified; they are not fuzzily joined to a different species. This is a display identity map, not a change to the game's catalogue or statistics.

Sources: [GBIF occurrence API](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/openapi/v1/occurrence), [GBIF taxonomy API](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/openapi/v1/species), [GBIF data-use guidance](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/data-use/), [BTO BirdFacts](https://www.bto.org/learn/about-birds/birdfacts). The existing national fixture retains its source provenance.
