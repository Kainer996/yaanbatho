# Village texture credits

Ground detail textures used by the 3D village renderer:

- `grass_c.jpg` / `grass_n.jpg` — derived from **Grass005** (color × baked AO, normal GL)
- `cobble_c.jpg` / `cobble_n.jpg` — derived from **PavingStones119** (color × baked AO, normal GL)

Both source materials are from [ambientCG.com](https://ambientcg.com), released
under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain — no attribution required, credited
here anyway). Files were downscaled to 512px, luminance-normalised, and the
ambient-occlusion maps were baked into the color maps for delivery size.
