## Explainer brief (paste this)

Explain the game Burbz — what it is, the story behind it, and how you play it.

Burbz is a birdwatching adventure you play by walking outside. Your phone's
microphone is a wizard's wand. It listens to real birdsong. Every bird it
confirms joins your flock as a hand-drawn manga fighter.

Start with the story. Burbz is the name of the enemy, and the Z is for zombie.
On a parallel Earth ruled by birds, a usurper raised a zombie flock — dead
bird-shapes with burning red eyes — and swallowed the kingdom in shadow. The
last free birds gathered around a falcon wizard called Merlin. His final spell
threw his spell-tablet across the multiverse. It landed in our world as your
phone. You are the one he chose.

Then show how it plays:

- Walk outside. Listen. A confirmed song unlocks a real species — hundreds of
  them, drawn from real science.
- Feed and train your birds. Every bird eats what it really eats in the wild. A
  kingfisher wants fish. A goldfinch wants seed. Offer the wrong food and it
  turns its beak up.
- Reach a real town on the map and the zombie Burbz stand in your way. Win the
  battle and the darkness lifts off that patch of the real world.
- Build a sanctuary birdhouse to hold the ground. Freed villages grow into
  towns. Towns unite into counties, kingdoms, an empire.
- Twenty walking quests carry the story, told by named birds who meet you at the
  trailhead and again at the end of the road.

Close on the promise: the map is Earth — your Earth, dark until you walk it.
Travel somewhere new and a new piece of the world wakes up.

End on this line, on screen and spoken: **Burbz. Go outside. Take the kingdom
back.** burbz.app

Tone: warm, a little mythic, never cutesy. Short sentences. Let the dark shots
sit for a beat before the light comes back.

## Shot list

Give the tool these beats if it accepts a scene breakdown.

1. **Cold open — the real world.** A phone on a hedgerow path at dawn, a real
   robin singing. Sound waves ripple out of the screen.
2. **The turn.** The waveform becomes a spell. The screen glows gold.
3. **The enemy.** The zombie Burbz — ragged black bird-shapes, red eyes, smoke
   for feathers — over a darkened Earth. Hold this shot. This is the villain.
4. **Merlin.** A falcon wizard in a ruined roost, casting a tablet across a
   star-field. It lands as a phone in a human hand.
5. **The listen.** Walking POV, phone up, birdsong bars rising, a species name
   resolving, a manga bird card flipping into the flock.
6. **The flock.** A great tree academy full of birds — kitchen, hospital,
   training hall. Warm, lived-in, Ghibli-ish.
7. **The battle.** Player's birds versus a squad of evil Burbz at the edge of a
   dark village.
8. **The liberation.** Shadow peels off a map of real Earth. A green ring of
   daylight opens around a town. Parchment land and inked town names underneath.
9. **The empire.** Rings of light spread and fuse — village, town, county,
   kingdom. Gold trade roads arc across the globe.
10. **End card.** Burbz logo on black. "Go outside. Take the kingdom back."
    burbz.app

## Facts to keep straight

- Burbz names the **enemy**. The player's own birds are never called Burbz —
  they are birds and companions.
- The species data is real. 758 species in the catalogue, real diets, real
  songs.
- The app is a static offline-first PWA at burbz.app. No server to go down.
- Battles free a **village**. Towns are made by merging freed villages.
