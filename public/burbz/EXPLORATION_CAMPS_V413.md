# Exploration and camps

Feature branch: `codex/exploration-camps`, based on public main `b30621188d9472e6621ced055558f5bf5ca74aa4`. Publication belongs to the established release owner after the art and Fireball releases. Runtime module pin: `exploration-camps-v413-20260914`. Global build/cache promotion is pending integration.

Walking and flying reveal approximately 90 metres of nearby terrain along actual movement. The save stores sparse 32×32 bit tiles at geographic Mercator zoom 19; both the realm darkness canvas and the local minimap read those tiles. No interpolated trail joins teleports. A 500 km synthetic journey used 95,709 bytes across 353 tiles; repeated exploration does not grow the save. Viewport culling and a 128-entry raster cache keep drawing bounded. Normal geographic position saves also commit exploration; visibility loss and ordinary exit flush it.

The Camps control appears beside the first-person map and in Home, the realm atlas and the real-life map. Pitch camp places a real tent four metres ahead on the ground, even during flight. The full tent, future house and arrival footprint must have loaded, clear, gentle ground. Tents are free. Water, structures and tree trunks reject placement with an explanation. Saved camps have UUIDs and canonical latitude/longitude; positions use the existing geographic projection without inventing GPS data. No sharing, placement progression lock, travel cooldown or travel payment is added.

Camps remain fast-travel destinations before a fire exists. Build a campfire beside a tent for **5 timber**, from `gameState.player.branches`, using the normal tree-chopping supply. Two ordinary home/village trees yield six timber. The camp sheet links to the primary home clearing for gathering and back to the saved camp. Fire construction and the timber deduction share the durable save, with rollback on failure and a saved `fire` flag against repeat taps. The fire uses actual logs, a stone ring and three small flame meshes; reduced-motion disables flicker, and no new frame loop or dynamic point light is created.

Up to twelve nearby campsite models share the existing walking scene. They unload at distance and rebuild from the same saved coordinates on return. Tent supports, fire details and lanterns sample actual terrain. Houses reuse `BurbzPlayerHomeScene.createHouse`; owned outdoor furniture and crops use the existing models, and the normal house controller supplies interiors, decoration, paid upgrades and rooms. Camp homes have separate saved home records. The primary `playerHome` remains untouched. Home exits use the selected house's own geographic anchor.

Real visits are accepted only through `handleLivePosition`, using `BurbzGeographicPlacesCore.arrival`: **within 45 m**, reported accuracy **50 m or better**, timestamp no older than **120 seconds** and no more than **10 seconds in the future**. The fix must also be at or after camp creation. Avatar movement, map panning, home anchors and virtual travel never call this visit path. The app uses the same client GPS authority as existing real-life quest doors; this is not an added server attestation system.

A qualifying real visit atomically saves its receipt, eligibility and **500 coins + 250 XP + 50 timber**. This is substantially larger than normal walking-story rewards (early stories award 60–180 coins and 60–200 XP). Normal level-up grants remain part of the same mutation. A previously rewarded place within **90 m** cannot yield another bonus, even from a distinct tent, but those tents can still become homes after a qualifying visit. Camp placement itself only excludes overlapping footprints within 12 m. After the visit is saved, **Build house · Free** converts that camp into a persistent additional cottage. Failed reward/home saves restore the previous state and can be retried. No network connection or paid service is added for physical verification.

## Validation

`tests/test_exploration_camps_v413.cjs` exercises movement/revisit persistence, dateline behavior, absence of a teleport corridor, current GPS gates, per-camp and per-place reward deduplication, actual app save rollback, timber accounting, reload, additional-home ownership, 3D geometry/disposal and exact three-list offline pins. Relevant existing geography, world and home/farming tests pass. New browser harness: `tests/run_exploration_camps_v413.cjs`, using the existing deterministic DEM/MVT fixture and real browser renderer. Browser and public verification are still in progress; do not treat this draft as release acceptance.
